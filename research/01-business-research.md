## Their work (what is visible)

Very little of the portfolio is online. Of the 70 Justdial photos, roughly 60 are the storefront, signboards and interior. Actual work appears only as:
- Window posters: Indian bridal portraits (red/gold saree, heavy jewellery), couple portraits, a pre-wedding collage with outdoor and backwater shots.
- IndiaMART (4 images): a bridal album page with rainbow-gradient digital effects, a girl in a yellow pavadai (half-saree / coming-of-age function), a black-and-white wedding couple in front of a street backdrop, and a couple portrait (bride in red, groom in a suit).
- Passport photo sheets.

### Style read
- Traditional South Indian wedding work with heavy album-style edits: gradients, colour splashes, collage layouts. This is the 2015–2020 "digital album" look.
- Some candid and B&W pieces show a move toward a cleaner, more modern style.
- Christian family-owned business (Maranatha, cross, Jesus portrait in the studio). Church weddings (white saree + garland, groom in a suit) are likely a real segment.
- Brand look is bright mass-market Telugu signage: black backgrounds, yellow/orange/red lettering.

## Market position

- Neighbourhood studio. The everyday business is passport photos, photostat and xerox. Weddings and functions are the high-value side.
- Competitors on the same Justdial page have far more social proof: Rafee Rey Candid (4.9, 161 ratings), Dream9 (5.0, 113), Zakir Photography (5.0, 101), Picture Skull (4.8, 66). Kalamkrutha has 2 ratings.
- Pricing is in the affordable mid-range for Guntur weddings.

## Gaps a website can fix

1. No portfolio online. This is the biggest problem: competitors win on visible work.
2. No reviews or testimonials.
3. No clear package presentation. Prices only exist on Justdial.
4. No WhatsApp-first enquiry flow, which is how Guntur customers actually book.
5. No Telugu + English bilingual presence.
6. Two businesses are mixed together (walk-in passport/xerox and event photography). The site has to present both without cheapening the wedding side.
